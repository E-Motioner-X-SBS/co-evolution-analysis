\section{Results: Rank Fusion of DCA and Karnaugh-Map Prior}
\label{sec:results-fusion}

\subsection{Hypothesis}
The Karnaugh-map cell-rate prior improves global ranking (AUC $0.658$) while
degrading top-$K$ sharpness relative to raw DCA (AUC $0.583$).  We
hypothesised that fusing the two signals by rank aggregation captures both
properties.

\subsection{Fusion Rules}
Five label-free fusion rules are evaluated on held-out proteins under the same
five-fold protein-level cross-validation as PATH~B.  Each rule combines a DCA
score with the Level-H cell-rate score (cells: residue groups $\times$
separation $\times$ DCA quartile, trained on the other folds):

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
	\item \textbf{ranksum}: $\mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{DCA}) + \mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{prior})$
	\item \textbf{zsum}: $z(\mathrm{DCA}) + z(\mathrm{prior})$
	\item \textbf{product}: $z(\mathrm{DCA}) \times \mathrm{prior}$
	\item \textbf{geometric}: $\sqrt{\mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{DCA}) \times \mathrm{rank}(\mathrm{prior})}$
	\item \textbf{DCA-gated}: $\mathrm{DCA}$ shifted by signed prior magnitude
	      depending on whether the prior is above or below its median.
\end{enumerate}

Re-running the published experiment on regenerated DCA caches reproduces it
to three decimals: rank-sum fusion reaches $0.204$ against $0.169$ for the
DCA score alone (paired difference $+0.036$, 95\% protein-bootstrap interval
$+0.017$ to $+0.054$, Wilcoxon $p = 1.8\times10^{-5}$).

The DCA score used there, however, is defective.  Its pair frequencies are
weighted by $W_n^3$ (the Gram matrix is built from one-hot vectors already
multiplied by $W_n$ and then by $\sqrt{W_n}$) while its single-site
frequencies use $W_n$, and its Frobenius norm is taken in the gauge left by the
inversion rather than in the zero-sum gauge that APC-Frobenius scoring
assumes~\cite{ekeberg2013}.  The result, $0.169$, is not better than
APC-corrected MI ($0.189$, $p = 0.24$).  A standard mean-field DCA on the same
alignments reaches $0.566$ (direct information) and $0.692$ (zero-sum-gauge
APC-Frobenius), close to PSICOV's $0.727$.

Against a working DCA the fusion does the opposite of the published claim.
Rank-sum fusion with the K-map prior lowers precision@L/5 from $0.566$ to
$0.471$ for DI ($-0.095$, interval $-0.116$ to $-0.073$, worse on 108 of 135
proteins that change) and from $0.692$ to $0.565$ for the Frobenius score
($-0.127$, interval $-0.151$ to $-0.103$).  The fusion raises AUC slightly
(0.758 to 0.767), so the prior helps order the bulk of candidate pairs, but it
costs the top of the ranking, which is what contact prediction uses.  The
drop is not specific to the prior: equal-weight rank aggregation of DI with
MIp lowers it too ($0.520$).  Rank aggregation with a much weaker score
dilutes a strong one.  The published gain therefore reflects a weak DCA input
being partly compensated, not information that DCA lacks.

\subsection{Mechanistic Decomposition}
An earlier version reported that the fusion rescues 264 contacts and loses
185, with every rescued pair above the prior's median.  The analysis behind
those counts is not in the repository and could not be re-derived; we
withdraw them.

\subsection{Three-Way Fusion}
Adding plain MI as a third signal to the published two-way fusion does not
change it (rank-3-way $0.201$ vs rank-2-way $0.204$, $p = 0.607$).
