\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

We have demonstrated that the analytical chain from biological sequences
through Gray-code encoding, Karnaugh-map construction, Quine--McCluskey
minimisation, and prime-implicant extraction can be made machine-provable in
Lean~4, and that the resulting Boolean circuits carry genuine structural
information when applied to protein multiple sequence alignments.

The principal finding is that the Karnaugh-map cell-rate prior carries
orthogonal structural information demonstrable by rank-fusion with continuous
DCA scores.  The fused predictor achieves prec@L/5 = 0.204, exceeding both
the circuit alone (0.124) and DCA alone (0.169) at $p < 10^{-4}$.  This is
the first evidence that Boolean-minimisation features improve continuous
coevolutionary contact prediction.

The biological interpretation of learned circuit rules confirms known
structural principles.  Level-1 essential implicants encode hydrophobic-core
packing, aromatic stacking, and sulfur-mediated contacts in directly readable
sum-of-products form.  At Level-2 resolution, specific residue-pair
vocabularies correspond to aliphatic/aromatic packing interactions that
dominate the buried cores of globular proteins.

Two results reframe what the rest of the paper reports.  The ceiling theorem
(Section~\ref{sec:res-ceiling}) converts an empirical shortfall into a bound:
because a circuit is a function of its feature cell, its expected precision is
capped by that cell's purity, and on consensus residue identity with separation
the cap is $0.193$ against the $0.179$ we achieve.  The circuit is therefore at
$93\%$ of what its inputs permit, and the $3.7\times$ distance to published DCA
is a property of the features rather than of the minimisation.  This settles a
question the earlier sections could only pose: no better minimiser, threshold,
don't-care policy or Boolean representation can close it, and effort is better
spent on the inputs.  It also explains why coarsening residues into eight
physicochemical groups \emph{lowers} the ceiling---merging cells can only
discard information---so chemical classes should be understood as buying
readability, not accuracy.

The second is a change of substrate rather than of method.  Boolean
minimisation is productive elsewhere in biology where the object is a logical
relation among variables observed across many states~\cite{kocevar2024sailor};
a static residue string is not such an object, whereas a structure-based-model
trajectory is.  Applied to conformational state
(Section~\ref{sec:res-contact-logic}), the same exact minimiser finds coupling
beyond the folding coordinate in 463 of 480 proteins, and---the substantive
point---the rules it learns are \emph{spatially local}: selected predictors are
$12.5\times$ enriched for contact-map adjacency in 478 of 480 proteins, with
more than a quarter sharing a residue with their target.  Conditional mutual
information, with the global folding mode controlled out, recovers
$\beta$-ladder registers and local packing units without being told that
secondary structure exists.  Under dense re-sampling this becomes a kinetic
statement: three neighbouring contacts predict a target contact's state a
thousand integration steps ahead, in every protein tested.

Several limitations constrain the scope of our conclusions.  Consensus-residue
features collapse within-column frequency variation into a single code,
discarding information that richer encodings might exploit.  Separation bins
use globally defined boundaries without per-protein length normalisation.
Cross-dataset generalisation was inconclusive on EVmutation due to A2M format
parsing complexity, leaving open the question of whether findings replicate
beyond PSICOV150.  The iterative direct-information computation from our
mean-field implementation remains unreliable on families with many fully
conserved columns; we adopted APC-corrected Frobenius norm as the primary DCA
score for this reason.  In the contact-state analysis the mean gain over the
$Q$-only baseline is negative at the production trajectory length, a
variance limit we diagnose by varying the predictor count and confirm by
re-simulation; the ten proteins re-simulated were chosen to be small and
strongly fluctuating, so their absolute levels are optimistic relative to the
480-protein population even though the paired comparisons are not.  Finally,
prime-implicant counts reported from the dense reference minimiser are lower
bounds rather than exact Quine--McCluskey quantities, for the reason given in
Section~\ref{sec:methods-contact-logic}.

Four extensions follow naturally.  Extending the dense re-sampling from ten
favourably chosen proteins to a representative sample of the 480 would
establish whether the sign flip holds at the population level, and would make
the kinetic analysis available at scale rather than as a demonstration.  Replacing binary conservation flags with
continuous entropy bins as additional Karnaugh-map dimensions could capture
finer-grained structural information about the buried core.  Integrating
circuit-derived rules as input features to deep learning architectures would
combine the interpretability of Boolean logic with the representational power
of attention-based models.  Applying the complete pipeline to CASP
free-modelling targets would test whether the observed transfer generalises
to harder benchmarks with shallower alignments.
